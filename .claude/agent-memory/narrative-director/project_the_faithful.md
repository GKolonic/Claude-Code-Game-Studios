The Faithful is a mobile narrative strategy game (portrait, touch, 10-20 min sessions, Godot 4.6) built on a two-scale architecture: a village-level conversion layer (the core experience) and a macro-layer Divine Dominion mode (region control).

**Core player fantasy (approved):** "The Weight of a Single Soul" — reverence, intimacy, the prophet who wins one person at a time. Every conversion is a small miracle and a small theft.

**Game Pillars (all four locked):**
- Pillar 1: Every Soul Has a Story — NPCs are characters, not resources
- Pillar 2: Many Roads to the Divine — multiple valid approaches (GRIEF / AMBITION / DOUBT / FEAR)
- Pillar 3: The Arc Must Feel Earned — slow, organic, full of setbacks
- Pillar 4: History Writes Itself — past decisions shape future states permanently

**Win/lose:** 50%+ of all world regions simultaneously (win); all regions below 10% conversion (lose).

**Why:** Two-scale architecture was the resolution of a MAJOR REVISION NEEDED verdict on the Divine Dominion GDD — the macro layer was retrofitted onto the village core, not designed first.

**How to apply:** All narrative guidance should reinforce the intimacy and weight of individual NPC encounters. The macro layer is strategically load-bearing but narratively secondary — don't let it flatten the village-level human drama.

**Current stage:** Pre-production. 6/14 MVP GDDs complete. Working on Dialogue & Conversion System GDD (system #7).
